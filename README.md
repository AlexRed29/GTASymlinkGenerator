## What is a "Symlink"?
**"Symlinks"**, in a nutshell, are like **"Advanced Shortcuts".** <br>

But, unlike conventional Shortcuts, the **Operating System (Windows/Linux)** will perceive it as if it were the original file or folder, although in reality it is just a link that points to them (Symbolize). <br>

In short, let's say that **"Links"** are like **"Portals"**. <br>
A portal that is constantly sending data from **"Point A"** to ***"Point B"**, **"Point C"**, **"Point D"***, etc. <br> <br>

### What are they used for?
**Symlinks** are used mainly to **save disk space**, by not having to Copy/Paste the same data to another location. <br>

They are also used to **save a lot of time**. If our HDD/SSD is slow at copying and pasting files, we can use **"Symlinks"** to instantly "copy" the same folder with files to another location with just a few commands. <br> <br>


### How can we use them in "Grand Theft Auto" (III/Vice City/San Andreas)?
If you are someone who is used to having many installations of your game (Be it for mods), you are surely tired of creating many copies of your same game in another location that consume a lot of space on your HDD/SSD. <br>

You literally Copy and Paste the same files **(Anim, Audio, Models)** in another location, even if you are not going to modify them. <br>

With Symbolic Links we can save a lot of storage space! <br> <br> <br>

# IMPORTANT! VERY VERY IMPORTANT!
### DO NOT make a "GTA Symlink" for Total Conversion mods!
**Total Conversion** mods are known for replacing the **MOST IMPORTANT GTA files** (***"Anim", "Audio", "Data", "Models"***). <br>

**Examples of these mods:**
- GTA Frosted Winter
- GTA Long Night
- GTA San Andreas Stories (SAS)
- GTA Carcer City
-Zombie Andreas
- Project Eagle (Stars & Stripes)
- GTA Forelli Redemption <br> <br>

If we use "Symlinks" for Total Conversion mods, **WE WILL DAMAGE** the files of our **Original (No mods)** installation, and therefore, we will damage the other **"GTA Symlink"**. <br>

Fortunately **"GTA Symlink Generator"** warns us about this at the beginning.

For **Total Conversion** mods, **YES OR YES** we are going to have to copy and paste the complete GTA. <br> <br>
